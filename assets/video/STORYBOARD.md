## Frame 1 — Hook
- duration: 9s
- scene: off-white ground; the phone rises into frame on "phone"; the surface idles with a YouTube track playing.
- voiceover: Laptop's plugged into the TV… That's your remote now. Meet Edgepad.

## Frame 2 — Setup
- duration: 40s
- scene: Devices screen; laptop cards for the release files, Bluetooth pairing and the "Trust …?" question (Trust pressed on "yes"); the PIN pad with the laptop's three security levels.

## Frame 3 — Trackpad
- duration: 18s
- scene: camera pushed in; a glide with its tail, a tap, a drag; two fingers scroll and pinch; three and four fingers swipe; Settings › Trackpad scrolls to the gestures.

## Frame 4 — Corner dials
- duration: 35s
- scene: the camera visits each corner as it is named (volume, brightness, zoom, scrub) with the laptop's level overlay; marks light up; Settings › Corners, Split corners flips and four dials become eight; the laptop's volume flyout moves and the dial follows.

## Frame 5 — Now playing and Lock
- duration: 15s
- scene: pause, play, skip to the next track, drag the piece in Media layout; two taps on the lock, the caption pill, only the dials answer.

## Frame 6 — Controls
- duration: 45s
- scene: Controls tiles; the phone turns sideways for the keyboard (Ctrl latched, Shift held) and the gamepad, back upright for Annotate (strokes appear on the laptop's screen), Sign (saved as an image) and Capture (Snipping Tool region).

## Frame 7 — Macros
- duration: 20s
- scene: the 13-macro deck; Edit layout, Spotify held and dragged to the front, a grid picked; a shape drawn on the trackpad starts Spotify.

## Frame 8 — Make it yours and trust
- duration: 30s
- scene: Corners with eight halves; light theme, a gradient background, sideways; the tray menu (Security…, Forget trusted phones); Windows, macOS, Linux standing.

## Frame 9 — CTA
- duration: 10s
- scene: No account / No server / No internet; end card with the mark, "Edgepad", "Free & open source." and the GitHub button.
