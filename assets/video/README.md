The 4.0.0 marketing videos: a HyperFrames project, rendered by the `video` job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. Nothing here runs locally.

- `scripts/<id>.json`: the narration, in chapters and lines. `full` (about 4.5 min, 16:9) and `short` (about 60 s, shared by the two social cuts).
- `voice.py`: one edge-tts take per script (`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`), cut at the line breaks and laid out with set pauses. It writes `out/<id>/voice.mp3`, `timing.json` (every word's time, line times, chapter starts, the poster frame), `timing.js` (the same, for the page) and `<id>.en.vtt`.
- `music.py`: an original music bed for each script, written from the script's chapter energy, so no licence or credit applies. It writes `out/<id>/music.mp3` at the cut's exact length. Each composition carries it as an `<audio id="music">` clip on track 1 at `data-volume="0.2"`, beside the voice on track 0; the bed is already ducked under speech in the file.
- `index.html` is the `full` composition (1920 x 1080). `cuts/` holds `linkedin` (1080 x 1440, 3:4) and `instagram` (1080 x 1920, 9:16), both cut from the `short` script, and the two covers, `thumb-landscape` (1280 x 720) and `thumb-portrait` (1080 x 1920). Each is a standalone composition rendered with `render -c`. A cut's `window.CUT` separates its composition id (`id`, which keys `window.__timelines`) from the narration it uses (`script`); the layout (16:9 side by side, 9:16 stacked, 3:4 stacked and tighter) comes from the canvas size.
- `ui/beats.js` says what each line shows: its headline (one keyword in blue), its sub-line and an act (camera moves, screens, fingers, laptop cards) keyed to the spoken words, one entry per line of the script. `ui/film.js` turns that into one paused GSAP timeline: the text is DOM revealed word by word, and the hook's dark room, the glow, the phone and the laptop cards are one canvas redrawn from a proxy tween's `onUpdate`, so every frame is a pure function of time.
- The look is dark: ground `#1E2026`, ink `#F2F3F5`, grey `#9A9CA3`, blue `#4DA3FF`, the caption pill `#2B2D33`, and the white tile `brand/edgepad-light.svg` as the mark. The phone keeps the app's own dark theme (`#0E0F12`), a darker object on a lighter room, lifted by a rim light and a slow blue glow that swells at each chapter start.
- `ui/kit.js`, `ui/surface.js` and `ui/screens.js` are the phone's screens, ported from the Android drawing code; each block names the Kotlin it follows. `icons/` holds the Simple Icons marks for the macro deck (see `icons/NOTICE`); `brand/` the app's Lato, the Edgepad marks and the app's own YouTube and Spotify logos.
- A still for checking by eye, with no narration run: open `index.html?line=13&f=0.4` (line 13, 40 % through) in Chrome with `--allow-file-access-from-files`; it times the words from the script instead.
- The job runs only when something under `assets/video/` changed. The `edgepad-video` artifact holds `full`, `linkedin` and `instagram`, a web copy and poster of `full`, the two cover PNGs, each script's captions, timing and music, and a loudness reading of voice and music.
- The site's `/edgepad-demo.*` files are not replaced by the job; copy the ones wanted over by hand.
