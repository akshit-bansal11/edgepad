The Edgepad 5 film: a HyperFrames project, rendered by the `video` job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. Nothing here runs locally. `BRIEF.md`, `STORYBOARD.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `MOTION.md`, `GAME.md`, `SOUND.md`, `FACTS.md` and `PORT.md` are its pre-production; this file is how it is built.

- `scripts/<id>.json`: the narration, in chapters and lines. `demo5` (about 2.5 min, 16:9) and `reel5` (about 90 s, shared by the two social cuts). Beside the words a script may hold `gap` (seconds between two lines of a chapter), `holds` (line number from 1 to extra seconds of silence after it), `poster_line` (the line whose middle is the poster frame), `duck_hold` (the line whose hold the music drops under) and `fit` (a longer hold when the cut comes out under a length, a shorter gap when over). `scripts/<id>.cues.json` is that script's sound-effect cue list.
- `voice.py`: one edge-tts take per script (`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`), cut at the line breaks and laid out with set pauses. It writes `out/<id>/voice.mp3`, `timing.json` (every word's time, line times and holds, chapter starts, the poster frame), `timing.js` (the same, for the page) and `<id>.en.vtt`.
- `music.py`: an original music bed for each script, written from the script's chapter energy, so no licence or credit applies. It writes `out/<id>/music.mp3` at the cut's exact length, already ducked under speech, and a further 4 LU under the race hold.
- `sfx.py`: the sound effects, made by earshot (MIT, pinned in `SOUND.md`) from the cue sheet in `SOUND.md`, each with `--seed 0`, and laid out from `timing.json` and the cue list into `out/<id>/sfx.mp3`. The race's engine follows the same input table as the picture.
- Each composition carries three audio clips: the voice on track 0, the bed on track 1 at `data-volume="0.2"`, the effects on track 2.
- Five cuts: the long film in two shapes, `demo5.html` (1920 x 1080) and `cuts/demo5-portrait` (1080 x 1920), and the short one, cut from the `reel5` script, in three, `cuts/reel5-landscape` (1920 x 1080), `cuts/reel5-portrait` (1080 x 1920) and `cuts/reel5-34` (1080 x 1440). `cuts/` also holds the two covers, `thumb5-landscape` (1280 x 720) and `thumb5-portrait` (1080 x 1920). Each is a standalone composition rendered with `render -c`. A cut's `window.CUT` separates its composition id (`id`, which keys `window.__timelines`) from the narration it uses (`script`); the layout (16:9 side by side; 9:16 and 3:4 stacked, the laptop above, the headline band, the phone below) comes from the canvas size.
- `ui/beats.js` says what each line shows: its headline (one keyword in blue), its sub-lines and an act (camera moves, phone screens, the laptop's screen, fingers, cards) keyed to the spoken words, one entry per line of the script. `ui/film.js` turns that into one paused GSAP timeline: the text is DOM revealed word by word, and the room, the laptop, the phone and the cards are one canvas redrawn from a proxy tween's `onUpdate`, so every frame is a pure function of time.
- `ui/kit.js`, `ui/surface.js`, `ui/screens.js`, `ui/screens5a.js` and `ui/screens5b.js` are the phone's screens, ported from the Android drawing code at 1 unit = 1 dp; each block names the Kotlin it follows, and every string on the phone is the app's own. `ui/laptop.js` draws the laptop, what is on its screen, the room and the hand. `ui/game.js` is "Night Lap", the film's own racing scene: the whole race is worked out once into a table, and the phone's gamepad reads the same inputs. `ui/ident.js` is the animated mark, the same maths as `scripts/logo-motion.ps1`.
- The look is dark: ground `#1E2026`, ink `#F2F3F5`, grey `#9A9CA3`, blue `#4DA3FF`, the caption pill `#2B2D33`, and the white-tile mark. The tokens are `EP.TOK` in `ui/kit.js`. `icons/` holds the Simple Icons marks for the macro deck (see `icons/NOTICE`); `brand/` the app's Lato and the Edgepad marks.
- A still for checking by eye, with no narration run: open `demo5.html?line=8&f=0.4` (line 9, 40 % through; lines count from 0 here) in Chrome with `--allow-file-access-from-files`; it times the words from the script instead.
- The job runs only when something under `assets/video/` changed. The work is three jobs: `video-sound` (the take, the bed, the effects), `video-render` (one job per cut, side by side, all from that one take) and `video` (the rest). The `edgepad-video` artifact holds the five cuts at full quality and a small copy of each (`<cut>-small.mp4`, for sites with an upload limit), a web copy and poster of `demo5`, the two cover PNGs, each script's captions, timing, music and effects; the job's log prints the loudness of each stem and of each finished mix. `edgepad-video-sheets` holds contact sheets, a frame every two seconds.
- The 4.0 film's files (`index.html`, `cuts/linkedin.html`, `cuts/instagram.html`, the `thumb-*` covers, `scripts/full.json`, `scripts/short.json`) are still on disk and are no longer rendered.
- The site's `/edgepad-demo.mp4`, `-poster.jpg` and `.en.vtt` (in `site/public/`) and `assets/demo-poster.jpg` are not replaced by the job; copy the artifact's web copy, poster and captions over them by hand.
