# The file-name sanitiser every app holds a received name to: one pair per line, "input | expected".
# Split each line at its LAST " | ". Lines starting with # and blank lines are skipped. The expected text (rejected) means
# the name is refused. Rules, in order: keep the last segment after / or \; drop control characters and invisible format characters (Unicode Cf: bidi marks and overrides, zero-width, BOM); replace < > : " / \ | ? *
# with _; trim trailing dots and spaces; cut to 120 UTF-8 bytes keeping an extension of at most 20 bytes; refuse an empty
# result and a Windows device name (CON PRN AUX NUL COM1-9 LPT1-9), with or without an extension. A clash is renamed
# "name (1).ext" by the receiver, never overwritten. Pairs hold no control characters, which the tests cover separately.
